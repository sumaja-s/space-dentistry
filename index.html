What Is Space Dentistry?

An emerging field that focuses on maintaining oral health before, during, and after spaceflight. It integrates dentistry, aerospace medicine, human physiology, engineering, and research.

Mission Safety

Oral health is a critical component of astronaut health and overall mission readiness.

Preventive Focus

Pre-launch dental screening and treatment reduce the risk of in-flight emergencies.

Scientific Research

Studies examine how spaceflight affects saliva, the oral microbiome, teeth, gums, and bone.

Oral Health Risks in Space

The space environment creates unique difficulties for oral hygiene and dental management. Insights drawn from recent review literature.

Microgravity

Fluid shifts and altered physiology influence oral tissues, salivary flow, and the feasibility of dental procedures.

Radiation

Cosmic radiation exposure is a significant consideration for long-duration missions and tissue health.

Oral Microbiome

Changes in microbial composition may increase risks related to biofilm, caries, and periodontal disease.

Limited Resources

Missions operate with constrained water, instruments, medications, and no immediate access to specialists.

Preflight Dental Care

Spaceflight dental planning prioritizes prevention, early diagnosis, and completion of necessary treatment before launch.

~6 months prior

Comprehensive dental examination and risk assessment.

~3 months prior

Completion and review of required dental treatment.

Before quarantine

Final oral-health review and emergency-care preparation.

During mission

Daily oral hygiene, monitoring, and approved protocols.

The Future of Space Dentistry

Future Moon and Mars missions will require new technologies for diagnosis, prevention, and treatment far from Earth.

AI Diagnosis

Artificial intelligence to support remote oral-health assessment and early detection.

Tele-Dentistry

Earth-based dentists guiding astronauts through selected procedures in real time.

3D Printing

On-demand fabrication of selected dental tools and temporary restorative devices.

Robotic Support

Robotic systems assisting with dental care during deep-space missions.
